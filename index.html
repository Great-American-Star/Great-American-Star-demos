Mathematical Foundation

The Great American Star arranges the phrase "UNITEDSTATESOFAMERICA250" into a five-pointed star using a modified mathematical structure. Each of the 359+ million paths represents a unique journey through the letter sequence, symbolizing individual American stories while forming a unified whole.

The Numbers Have Meaning

  • 50 starting points.
  • 50 ending points.
  • 359,277,250 unique mathematical paths.
  • One American star.

The 50 starting points and 50 ending points represent the 50 states.

Between them are 359,277,250 possible mathematical paths — designed around the idea of a unique path for every American.

The patriotic symbolism is not simply added to the artwork.
It is embedded in the mathematics.

Freedom 250 Connection

This project celebrates America's 250th anniversary through the principle of "E Pluribus Unum" (Out of Many, One). The mathematical structure creates exactly the right number of paths to represent individual Americans, making this both an artistic tribute and a symbolic representation of American unity in diversity.

The design celebrates individual journeys while highlighting how millions of unique paths converge to form a single, beautiful star that represents our nation's enduring spirit of freedom and unity.
