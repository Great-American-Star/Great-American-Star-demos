A Large-Scale Interactive Experience

Imagine The Great American Star transformed into a large interactive installation — on a wall, across a floor, or as part of an immersive exhibit.

Visitors could explore the mathematics at full scale, choose an individual path, and physically follow UNITED STATES OF AMERICA 250 from the center of the star toward one of its points.

The concept can combine mathematics, art, technology, America 250, and personal participation in an experience designed for museums, science centers, public exhibitions, and other large-scale venues.
